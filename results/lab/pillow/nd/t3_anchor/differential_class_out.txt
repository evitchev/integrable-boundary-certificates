(3) VERDICT
   A single flat family over the FULL range cannot match the deficit (that is what closed the multiplication-operator class).
   A SUM of flat families over DIFFERENT ranges gives the edge monomials a different coefficient from the interior ones, which is
   exactly what the deficit does (pure coefficients with their own denominator and their own closed form).  So the DIFFERENTIAL
   class survives the flatness filter, and it is the class to fit next: its free data are the coefficients of the two (or more)
   ranges at each spin, tied across spins by the continued-Beta coefficients of the single-sector functionals.

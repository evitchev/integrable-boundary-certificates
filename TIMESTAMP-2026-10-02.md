**Statement.** For each of the three one-parameter families of commuting quantum integrals of motion of the cylindrical (free boson plus O(N) vector,
N = (t^2 - 25)/(t^2 - 1)) model, an explicit linear differential operator without free parameters has been constructed (orders 2, 3 and 4; equivalently
pseudo-differential operators with Gamma-function-ratio symbols), whose WKB local charges reproduce the certified vacuum eigenvalues exactly through spin 13-15,
including predictions registered before the comparison data were opened.

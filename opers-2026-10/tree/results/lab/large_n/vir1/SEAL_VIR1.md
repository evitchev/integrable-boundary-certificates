## 2. The object

V_w   = the Z2 x O(N)-even mixed space of weight w in the monomial basis
        (xs, yp) (dimensions 7 at w = 4, 20 at w = 6 by my count).
Vir_N = span of the states L_{-n1} ... L_{-nk} |0>, n1 >= ... >= nk >= 2,
        of T_Y = (1/2)(11)_Y, written in the Y-monomial basis by iterated
        regular OPE coefficients, L_{-n} A = coefficient of (z-w)^(n-2) in
        T_Y(z) A(w).  (Hand claim: this subspace is N-independent in the
        monomial basis, because a closed Y-loop needs both legs of T(z)
        contracted and then nothing regular in z remains.)
HV_w  = (even X-monomials) x Vir_N, total weight w  (Heis_X (x) Vir_N).
Q_w   = V_w / (HV_w + d V_{w-1}).   Counts by hand: dim Q_4 = 1 (the weight-4
        singlet W_4), dim Q_6 = 3 ((dX)^2 W_4, (T W_4), W_6).

spin 3, three at spin 5.  They are well defined up to GL(dim Q_w); the zero
set is basis-independent.  I shall fix the functionals by an explicit left
